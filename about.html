OUR STORY

Born from tradition.

A decade of celebrating India's culinary heritage.

THE BEGINNING

A kitchen built on memories.

Zaika began with a dream: to create a place where India's rich culinary heritage could meet the energy of modern India.

Our founder grew up surrounded by the aromas of cardamom, cinnamon, charcoal and freshly baked bread. Those childhood memories became the foundation of Zaika.

Today, every plate carries a little piece of that journey.
